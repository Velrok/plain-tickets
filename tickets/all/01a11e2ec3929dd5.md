## Problem

`created_at` and `updated_at` are bare `u64` epoch seconds (`domain/tickets.rs`). They are unreadable in the files and in `show`. The old implementation in `../main` stores ISO 8601.

## Acceptance

- A `Timestamp` newtype serialises to and deserialises from RFC 3339 UTC with millisecond precision, e.g. `2026-04-30T19:00:00.123Z`. Round-trip unit test.
  - Milliseconds matter: `list` orders by `created_at`, and the random-ID ticket removes the time-sorted ID used as the same-second tie-break.
- `Timestamp` replaces the `u64` fields and `now()` in `cli/handlers.rs`; `Ticket::new` and `revise` take it.
- Malformed timestamps give a clear error.
- Presenters and the README format notes are updated.

## Open decision

Old epoch values: reject with a clear error for now (suggested), or read them as a migration.
